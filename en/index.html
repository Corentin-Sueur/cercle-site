Who runs the French state, and where they come from and go

Le Cercle links every appointment to a minister's office, the Élysée, Matignon, the government and the boards of state companies published in the Journal officiel since 1990 with Parliament, the HATVP, Wikidata, the company register, the register of lobbies and public contracts. It shows who worked together, for how long, and what they did before and after.

Read this first

  • Built automatically from public records. Every name, date and role comes from a public source linked on the person's page. Nothing is entered by hand.
  • Matches can be wrong. Records from different sources are joined by rules: an official identifier, a Wikidata id, a full birth date, or a name with a birth month or a shared board. A namesake can slip through. Every link says how it was made; check the source before relying on it.
  • Appearing here says nothing wrong about anyone. Working in an office, sitting on a board, moving to a company or holding a role in an organisation that lobbies is lawful and common. The Leads page lists coincidences of dates and names, places to start checking, not findings or accusations.
  • Incomplete by nature. Moves to the private sector, family ties and company roles are known only when a source records them. A missing role does not mean there was none.
  • Dated. The data is a snapshot, refreshed from the sources from time to time; the date is at the top of every page.
