About Le Cercle

Where the data comes from, the terms it is reused under, how to ask for a correction, and who publishes the site.

Where the data comes from

Every source is public and open for reuse. The data is shown as the sources give it, linked back to them, with the day each source was retrieved below and the date of each file on the facts page. Personal data is reused within the GDPR: only what the sources themselves publish (never the parts of HATVP declarations the HATVP keeps private, such as relatives), no home addresses, and anything a source withdraws disappears at the next refresh.

Corrections and objections

If something about you is wrong, or you object to appearing here, write to . Say which page or person it is about. Errors are corrected, and objections are answered, within one month.

Personal data

This site publishes personal data taken from public sources: names, appointments, elected mandates, outside roles, and for some people a birth year or month, a year of death from INSEE's deaths file, and family ties recorded in Wikidata. It does so in the legitimate interest of informing the public about how the state is staffed and how its staff move to and from companies and lobbies (article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR). Parliamentary staff named in the declarations of interests and the Assemblée's and the Sénat's lists appear only when they also worked in a minister's office headed by the same deputy or senator. Visits are counted with GoatCounter, an open-source audience-measurement service that sets no cookie, stores no IP address and keeps only the page, the referrer, the browser and system, the country or region and the screen size of each visit; the counts are held by GoatCounter for the publisher alone. Page views, the profiles opened, clicks to sources and searches with no result (their length only, never the text typed) are also measured with PostHog, hosted in the European Union, without cookies: nothing is stored on or read from the device, visitors are counted by a fingerprint computed on PostHog's servers with a key renewed every day, no profile is created, sessions are not recorded and the IP address is not kept. The site has no accounts and no other tracking. The pages that name people ask search engines not to index them, so the site does not come up when someone searches a name.

You can ask to see, correct or remove what concerns you, and object to appearing here, at the address above. You can also complain to the CNIL (cnil.fr).
